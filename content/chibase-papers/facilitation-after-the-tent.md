The meeting is not the work. The work is what remains when the tent comes down.

Too many infrastructure programmes still treat public participation as an event: attendance register, photographs, a report that nobody can trace to a named owner. Six weeks later a grievance arrives on WhatsApp and nobody can prove who promised what.

Facilitation that holds:

- Before: a stakeholder map with place and influence — community, traditional authorities, local government, contractor.
- During: purpose, attendance, notes, and new concerns captured into the same case convention as the grievance desk.
- After: commitments with due dates; an acknowledgment cadence; a RACI that actually consults community reps and customary structures.

Chibase Consulting runs that practice. Operators who need the trail on a durable desk use TrustLedger as a separate SRM product — suggest, apply, save; humans still own the closure.

If your last consultation cannot be audited, it was hospitality, not participation.
