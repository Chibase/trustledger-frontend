Infrastructure does not move on a licence and a budget alone. It moves when host communities still grant permission after the first delay, the first dust complaint, and the first broken promise.

Social licence is operational risk. When it fails, delivery fails — mining, energy, public works, and municipal programmes alike.

Chibase Consulting is a social facilitation and advisory practice. We help programmes in South Africa and the Global South earn and keep a Social Licence to Build through:

1. **Facilitation that survives the tent** — named people, customary structures, commitments with owners.
2. **MEL that cites evidence** — grievances, engagements, and promises on one trail.
3. **IKS in the mainstream** — Indigenous Knowledge Systems as method, not folklore.

The desk that makes that trail auditable for operators is TrustLedger. Consulting and software stay separate: a facilitation engagement does not unlock a workspace, and a software plan is not a substitute for people on a live site.

Ask whether your next site meeting will still be reconstructable in six months. If the answer is a WhatsApp search, the licence is already thin.
