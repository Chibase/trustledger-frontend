Monitoring and evaluation for infrastructure and community-trust programmes should not begin at month-end with a blank slide pack.

A defensible MEL spine cites:

- Intake, SLA, severity, and closure from one case desk — not reconstructed chat.
- Engagements as events: who was consulted, including customary counterparts.
- Commitments as promises with dates, owners, and fulfilment or breach.
- Community-defined outcomes and IKS beside indicator sets. Lived experience is not a footnote.

Chibase Consulting’s MEL practice is built for that evidence path. Software that stores the trail for entitled operator plans is TrustLedger — independent of consulting invoices.

Funders and boards do not buy decoration. They buy an audit trail: what trust was, when it broke, who owned the fix, and whether the commitment held.
