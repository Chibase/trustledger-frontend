A case list is not Stakeholder Relationship Management.

SRM needs named people and institutions, engagements that happened, and commitments with due dates. That chain — Stakeholder → Engagement → Commitment — is the engine. TrustLedger runs it on TrustLedger Cloud for entitled plans (Project, Institutional, or add-ons).

Solo remains an honest entry grievance desk. Practitioner is the AI step-up. We do not silently unlock a full stakeholder CRM on the cheapest plan.

Without a registry you mainly have tickets. Without engagements you have no memory of who was in the room. Without commitments, promises evaporate.
