When grievances live in WhatsApp and spreadsheets, nobody can prove what happened.

No owner. No timeline. No evidence trail. Trust erodes quietly — until the project stops.

TrustLedger puts community concerns on a durable desk: intake → ownership → closure, with a trail leadership can inspect. Every line of a grievance should tell a story: reported, resource deployed, investigating, resolved, verified, closed.

That is the heart of the live product. AI Assist, where entitled, only suggests. A human applies. Then it saves.
