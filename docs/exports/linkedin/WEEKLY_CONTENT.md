## Week 3 — Stakeholder Intelligence (Version 002)

### Post 3A — Engine
A case list is not SRM.

Stakeholder Relationship Management needs named people and institutions, engagements that happened, and commitments with due dates.

That chain — Stakeholder → Engagement → Commitment — is the engine. TrustLedger runs it on TrustLedger Cloud for entitled plans.

→ [product]

### Post 3B — Without the chain
Without a stakeholder registry, you mainly have tickets.

Without engagements, you have no memory of who was in the room.  
Without commitments, promises evaporate.

TrustLedger connects all three so boards can ask: who did we meet, what did we promise, and did we keep it?

→ FAQ: [faq]

### Post 3C — Plans honesty
Stakeholder CRM, engagements, and commitments sit on Project and Institutional plans (or add-ons).

Solo and Practitioner stay honest: entry desk and AI step-up — not a silent unlock of full SI.

→ Plans on [marketing site] · Product: [product]

## Week 4 — AI Assist (human in the loop)

### Post 4A — Suggest → Apply → Save
AI should never close a community case on its own.

On TrustLedger, AI Assist suggests. A human applies. Then it saves.

That is how you keep auditability when social licence is on the line.

### Post 4B — Who gets AI
AI Assist is on Practitioner and above — for teams ready to move faster without giving up human judgement.
