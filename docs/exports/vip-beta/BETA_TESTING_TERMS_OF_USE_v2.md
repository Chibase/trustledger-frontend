## 1. Purpose

Chibase Consulting (“**Chibase**”, “**we**”) invites selected practitioners (“**Tester**”, “**you**”) to evaluate TrustLedger during an early beta. The goal is product learning — navigation, modules, and fit for Social Relations Management — **not** production deployment of live client programmes.

Access is complimentary for the stated period. It is **not** a paid subscription and does not create an obligation to purchase.

---

## 2. What you may use for testing (data rules)

### 2.1 Allowed test data

You **may** enter or upload only:

1. **Anonymised / fictional** project scenarios you invent for testing;  
2. **Mock** stakeholders, engagements, commitments, and cases with no real identifiers;  
3. **Historical or public-record** material that you are already free to use without client confidentiality constraints; and/or  
4. **Your own** non-confidential notes about process (e.g. “how I would structure a grievance desk”), without naming clients or beneficiaries.

### 2.2 Prohibited data

You **must not** enter or upload:

1. Confidential **client** project data covered by your professional or contractual confidentiality duties;  
2. **Personal information** of beneficiaries, community members, or other data subjects where you do not have a lawful basis and authority to place it on a third-party platform;  
3. Special-category / sensitive personal data (e.g. health, ethnicity linked to individuals, children’s data) unless you have explicit written authority and Chibase has agreed in writing for that specific case (default: **not permitted** in this beta);  
4. Credentials, bank details, or identity documents of third parties.

**Chibase does not require live client data to participate.** Declining to load real client files does **not** breach this agreement.

### 2.3 Your responsibility

You remain responsible for ensuring that any content you place in TrustLedger complies with your client contracts, professional rules, and applicable data-protection law (including POPIA in South Africa where it applies to you). When in doubt, use mock or anonymised data.

## 3. Data governance, privacy, and access

### 3.1 Who controls what

| Item | Position |
|------|----------|
| **Your Tester Content** (data you enter) | You retain ownership / control of rights in that content, subject to the limited licence below. |
| **TrustLedger platform, branding, software** | Owned by Chibase / its licensors. |
| **Feedback** (comments, suggestions, bug reports) | You grant Chibase a perpetual, royalty-free licence to use Feedback to improve TrustLedger **without** attributing confidential client facts (Feedback should not include prohibited data under §2.2). |

### 3.2 How Chibase uses Tester Content

Chibase will use Tester Content **only** to:

- Provide and operate your VIP beta workspace;  
- Secure, back up, and troubleshoot the service;  
- Improve TrustLedger product quality **in aggregate or after stripping personal identifiers**, where Feedback is involved.

Chibase will **not**:

- Sell Tester Content;  
- Share Tester Content with unrelated third parties for their marketing;  
- Use Tester Content to **train external foundation / large language models** operated by third parties;  
- Publish your identifiable client matters.

If AI Assist features are enabled on your package, suggestions remain **suggest → human apply → save**. Model providers used for product AI (when live) are configured under Chibase’s controls; beta default may still use mock / non-production AI paths. Prohibited data under §2.2 must still not be entered.

### 3.3 Who can access your workspace

Access is limited to:

1. **You** (Plan Owner) and people **you** invite under VIP guest rules (view + comment invitees where enabled);  
2. **Chibase Platform Operators** solely for support, security, entitlement, and beta operations (allowlisted ops accounts);  
3. Sub-processors that host the stack (e.g. Frappe Cloud for application data, Vercel for the app front end, email delivery for login codes) under Chibase’s arrangements — not for their independent use of your content.

A current high-level list of infrastructure hosts can be provided on request during the beta.

### 3.4 Isolation

Your VIP account is provisioned as a distinct Customer (named e.g. `VIP Pilot — …`) with entitlement **active** and **no Paystack billing**. It is not mixed into other customers’ commercial subscriptions for charging.

### 3.5 Security (honest beta standard)

Chibase applies industry-reasonable controls for an early production / beta system (access control, encrypted transport, operator allowlists). **No system is risk-free.** Do not place prohibited data (§2.2) in the beta. TrustLedger is provided **as-is** during beta without warranty of uninterrupted or error-free operation.

### 3.6 End of beta — retention and purge

At the end of your access period (or earlier if you withdraw):

1. Login access will be turned off (entitlement cancelled / user disabled);  
2. On written request to **info@trustledger.co.za** (or your Chibase contact), Chibase will **delete or irreversibly anonymise** Tester Content from the active Cloud workspace within a reasonable operational window (target: **30 days** of a clear purge request after access ends);  
3. Routine backups may persist for a limited backup cycle and then age out;  
4. Chibase may keep **non-identifying** Feedback and product metrics.

You may also request a mid-beta purge of specific content; we will confirm when complete.

## 4. Time commitment (flexible)

The VIP window is typically **up to 8 weeks**.  

**There is no minimum hours requirement.** You may:

- Take a casual look-through;  
- Test only the modules that matter to you;  
- Provide feedback only when you have capacity; or  
- Pause and return within the window.

We prefer honest, light participation over a signed promise you cannot keep. If your availability changes, tell us — we can shorten the window or pause access without penalty.

Periodic feedback requests are **optional**, not contractual quotas.

## 5. Confidentiality of the product

You agree not to publicly disclose non-public TrustLedger roadmap details, security weaknesses, or unpublished pricing experiments shared privately in the beta, except to your professional advisors under confidentiality, or as required by law.  

You **may** describe at a high level that you evaluated an SRM / grievance desk product, without sharing other testers’ data or Chibase’s unpublished commercial terms.
