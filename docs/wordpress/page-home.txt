      <h2 id="faq-title" class="section-header__title">TrustLedger — common questions</h2>

    <details class="faq__item" open>
      <summary class="faq__button">What is TrustLedger?</summary>
      <div class="faq__panel">TrustLedger is a Stakeholder Relationship Management (SRM) software platform for grievance resolution and Stakeholder Intelligence. It helps operators run community intake, case desks, stakeholder registries, engagements, commitments, and governance-grade reports on projects where social licence decides whether work moves.</div>
    </details>
    <details class="faq__item">
      <summary class="faq__button">What is Stakeholder Relationship Management (SRM) software?</summary>
      <div class="faq__panel">SRM software tracks the people and institutions around a project — stakeholders, engagements, commitments, and grievances — so operators can prove how trust issues were handled. TrustLedger is SRM software built for infrastructure, mining, energy, and public-sector programmes in developing regions.</div>
    </details>
    <details class="faq__item">
      <summary class="faq__button">How do I track community grievances securely with TrustLedger?</summary>
      <div class="faq__panel">Use TrustLedger’s Version 001 resolution desk: field or community issue intake, owned cases with evidence, stage timestamps from reported through closed, and reports that cite the case trail. Paying and trial workspaces use your own data — not fictional sample incidents. AI only suggests; a human applies before anything is saved.</div>
    </details>
    <details class="faq__item">
      <summary class="faq__button">Is TrustLedger suitable for local municipalities and public-sector projects in South Africa?</summary>
      <div class="faq__panel">Yes. TrustLedger supports South African geo/place context (ward and municipality), grievance desks, Stakeholder Intelligence, and board or funder reporting packs. Institutional is quote-based with programme packs for municipal / IDP, housing, infrastructure, and renewable. Start with the SRM readiness assessment or a 14-day own-data trial.</div>
    </details>
    <details class="faq__item">
      <summary class="faq__button">What is the difference between Version 001 and Version 002?</summary>
      <div class="faq__panel">Version 001 is the live grievance resolution desk (projects, incidents, human-applied AI assist on entitled plans, reports). Version 002 is Stakeholder Intelligence — registry, engagements, and commitments on TrustLedger Cloud for entitled plans (Project, Institutional, or add-ons). Version 002 is in active use and still deepening versus a full TEDS blueprint.</div>
    </details>
    <details class="faq__item">
      <summary class="faq__button">Does TrustLedger use AI to close grievance cases automatically?</summary>
      <div class="faq__panel">No. TrustLedger AI Assist only suggests next steps or wording. A human must apply the suggestion before anything is saved. Cases are never auto-closed by AI.</div>
    </details>
    <details class="faq__item">
      <summary class="faq__button">How do I start a TrustLedger trial?</summary>
      <div class="faq__panel">Open a 14-day trial with your own projects and cases. Learn features on the product overview. Subscribe on TrustLedger, or request a quote for Institutional. The sample demo desk is retired.</div>
    </details>
    <details class="faq__item">
      <summary class="faq__button">Which TrustLedger plan includes Stakeholder CRM and commitments?</summary>
      <div class="faq__panel">Project and Institutional plans include Stakeholder CRM, engagements, and commitments by default. Practitioner can add CRM or commitments via sellable add-ons. Solo is an entry grievance desk without AI Assist or Stakeholder CRM in the box.</div>
    </details>
    <details class="faq__item">
      <summary class="faq__button">Where does TrustLedger store live customer data?</summary>
      <div class="faq__panel">Live customer workspaces run on TrustLedger Cloud at app.trustledger.co.za. You use the TrustLedger product app for day-to-day work; marketing content lives on trustledger.co.za. Paying and trial workspaces never show fictional sample incidents.</div>
    </details>
    <details class="faq__item">
      <summary class="faq__button">What is Admin login?</summary>
      <div class="faq__panel">Admin / live login is for provisioned operators and Plan Owners after Cloud access is set up — not a substitute for the public trial path.</div>
    </details>
    <details class="faq__item">
      <summary class="faq__button">How do I buy a plan?</summary>
      <div class="faq__panel">Subscribe on TrustLedger for Solo, Practitioner, or Project. Institutional programmes are quote-led (municipal, housing, infrastructure, renewable).</div>
